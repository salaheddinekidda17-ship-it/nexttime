Common questions

Where are my cards kept?
Only on your iPhone. NextTime has no account and no servers. Your cards, photos and voice notes are included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup, and you can save a NextTime backup file from Settings › Your data and backups.

How much does NextTime cost?
Up to 10 active cards are free. A single in-app purchase, not a subscription, unlocks as many as you like. Archived cards don't count toward the 10.

I bought the unlock on another iPhone.
Open Settings › Plan in NextTime and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Place reminders don't appear.
In NextTime, open Settings › Place reminders and check that reminders are on, Location is set to “Always” and Notifications are allowed. The place also needs a location and at least one active card. Reminders are quiet from 10 pm to 7 am unless you turn that off.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?
Delete cards inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone.
