Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026. This policy covers the NextTime app for iPhone.

The short version

NextTime does not collect your data. Everything you put in the app stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and NextTime has no servers.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The cards you write: lessons, titles, notes, categories and place names.
  • Photos you attach. NextTime keeps its own resized copy, made without the original's hidden details such as where it was taken. It only sees the photos you choose and does not ask for access to your photo library.
  • Voice notes you record. The microphone is used only while you record one.
  • Place locations you set, and your preferences.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is included in the backups your iPhone makes to iCloud or a computer, under Apple's terms. If you choose to save a NextTime backup file, that file contains your cards, photos and voice notes and is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

Camera, microphone and location

iOS asks your permission before NextTime can use any of them, and you can change your answer in the Settings app at any time.

  • Camera: only when you choose to take a photo for a card.
  • Microphone: only while you record a voice note.
  • Location: only if you use “Use my current location” for a place, or turn on Place reminders. Reminders use your iPhone's region monitoring: iOS tells NextTime when you arrive at a place you chose, and NextTime shows a notification. NextTime does not record where you go, keep a location history, or send your location anywhere.

Services from Apple

Searching for a place uses Apple Maps; what you type in that search is handled by Apple under its privacy policy. Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. NextTime only learns whether the unlock has been bought.

Children

NextTime does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your information

Delete a card in the app to remove it and its photo and voice note. Deleting the app removes everything NextTime stored on your iPhone.
